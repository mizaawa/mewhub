## MewHub 1.9.2

### 空间直角坐标系：连线说明、延伸坐标轴与自适应 XY 网格

- 明确指导模型如何用命名点和线段表达多边形、三角形与向量，自动闭合轮廓，不擅自添加对角线。
- XYZ 坐标轴现在是贯穿当前画布的直线，不会终止在数据范围边界。
- XY 平面增加轻量网格底面；网格间距随视图缩放自动调整，在屏幕上保持稳定密度。
- 移除干扰坐标平面的立体盒框，并修正网格绘制范围。

### 3D Cartesian plots: clearer edges, extended axes and adaptive XY grid

- Explain point and segment construction explicitly so polygons and vectors use the requested edges, close their outlines and avoid unrequested diagonals.
- Extend the X, Y and Z axes as straight lines across the visible canvas instead of ending at data-range bounds.
- Add a subtle XY reference plane with grid spacing that adapts to zoom to keep screen density consistent.
- Remove the distracting wireframe cage and correct the grid bounds.
