## MewHub 1.9.4

### 坐标系绘图与夜间切页

- 修复夜间模式进入或返回页面时，转场误用浅色填充造成的整屏闪亮；转场和导航底层都跟随当前主题。保留原有切页动画和 iOS 侧滑返回。
- 平面图不再把陡峭的连续直线误判为断点；水平参考线直接绘制，并减少普通曲线的重复采样。
- 平面图与空间图统一识别全角公式、带符号的上标和多位数指数。空间图支持 `xy²` 这样的隐式乘法，并用同一套数学排版显示图例。
- 空间图的 `z = ±√(1 − x² − y²)` 同时绘出上下两支。旋转、平移、缩放时复用曲面采样；重叠曲面按相机深度而非输入顺序绘制。

### Plot rendering and dark navigation

- Use the active theme for the Android transition fill and the surface behind the navigator, preventing bright flashes without disabling transitions or iOS back gestures.
- Trace steep continuous lines without bridging poles, draw horizontal references directly, and avoid evaluating 2D samples twice.
- Both plots accept full-width equations and signed or multi-digit superscripts. Surface legends use the same math typography as curve legends and support adjacent variables such as `xy²`.
- Draw both branches of ± surfaces, reuse world-space samples during camera gestures, and depth-sort overlapping surfaces together.
