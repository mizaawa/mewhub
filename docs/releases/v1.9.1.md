## MewHub 1.9.1

### 空间直角坐标系：修复手势并改善显示

- 修复单指旋转不连续、双指平移距离异常，以及手指数变化时视图跳动的问题；聊天卡片和全屏页的操作方式一致。
- 在聊天消息滚动区域内拖动坐标系时，手势由图表接管，不再被外层滚动抢走。
- 按真实 x/y/z 比例绘制空间范围，并根据屏幕尺寸自动适配视图；改进网格、曲面和刻度的清晰度。
- 修复全负数坐标范围导致坐标轴绘制异常的问题。

### 3D Cartesian plots: gesture and rendering fixes

- Fixed discontinuous one-finger orbit, incorrect two-finger panning and camera jumps when fingers are added or lifted. The chat card and full-screen view now behave consistently.
- Drags on an inline plot are handled by the plot instead of the surrounding chat scroll view.
- Preserve the actual x/y/z scale, fit the view to the available screen and improve grid, surface and tick readability.
- Fixed axis rendering for ranges that contain only negative values.
